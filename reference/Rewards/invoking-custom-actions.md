Some example custom actions could be:

- Opening an app once a day
- Completing an on-boarding process
- Reading an article
- Watching a video

Invoked actions will only progress related quests if the application is configured for automatic quest-processing.  Talk your account manager to enable this feature.  If it is not enabled then automatic quest-processing will require explicitly starting the quest for every user.
