**Genie is LiveLike's AI operator for your engagement program.** Tell it what you want in plain English — "create a three-question quiz for tonight's game", "which quests had the highest completion last week?", "every Monday at 9am, post the weekly leaderboard to Slack" — and Genie plans the work, asks you to confirm anything that changes your app, and carries it out using the same LiveLike APIs your producers use in Producer Suite.

# How to Enable Genie

* Contact your Account Manager
* Email [support@livelike.com](mailto:support@livelike.com)
* Once we've confirmed that Genie is enabled navigate to:
  * Producer Suite → Business Tools → Genie
  * This will lead you to [genie.livelike.ai](https://genie.livelike.ai).

## What you can do with Genie

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build engagement** | Create and manage widgets (polls, quizzes, predictions), programs, quests, badges, rewards, leaderboards, streaks, sponsors, chat rooms, comment boards, moments and trivia — with Arcade mini-games coming soon. |
| **Answer questions with data** | Ask about engagement in plain language. Genie queries LiveLike Analytics and explains what it found. |
| **Moderate** | Review chat rooms and comment boards, classify content and manage user bans. |
| **Automate recurring work** | Turn any request into a **Task** that runs on a schedule — daily recaps, weekly leaderboard resets, pre-game content drops. |
| **Teach it your house rules** | Write a **Skill** once — a naming convention, required attributes, a tone of voice — and Genie applies it every time it's relevant. |
| **Reach your other tools** | Add **Connectors** (Slack, Asana, Segment, Sportradar and more) so Genie can read from and act in the services around LiveLike. |
| **Try what's next** | Turn on **Preview Settings** to test agents that are still in preview, without changing how Genie behaves for anyone else. |

## How Genie works

1. **You ask.** Type a request in the chat, attach files if they help, and send.
2. **Genie routes and plans.** An orchestrator picks the right specialist agent (Widgets, Quests, Analytics, a connector, and so on). For multi-step requests it builds a plan, and steps that don't depend on each other run together.
3. **You confirm.** When a decision is yours, Genie asks. Before it saves anything important, it shows you the plan and waits for you to reply **confirm**.
4. **Genie reports back.** You get a summary of what happened, with links to the resources it touched.

Every conversation is saved as a **session** you can return to, rename, or continue later.
