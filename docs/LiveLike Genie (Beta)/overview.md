## Key Capabilities

| **Capability**                | **What it means**                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Manage Resources**          | Create, edit, and publish Programs, Text Polls, Predictions, and Quizzes without leaving chat.                                             |
| **Real-time Internet Access** | Genie can fetch live data like scores, or headlines and use it inside prompts or widgets.                                                  |
| **Conversational Knowledge**  | Talk to the Genie in natural language. "Create 3 trivia questions on the greatest NBA players of all time" or "List all my live programs." |
| **Inline Feedback**           | Give quick feedback using thumbs up/down icons or type `feedback:` followed by your comment.                                               |

***

## Enable the Genie

### Prerequisites

* Access to the **LiveLike Producer Dashboard**
* At least one **live application**

### How to Enable Genie

* Contact your **Account Manager**, or
* Email: [support@livelike.com](mailto:support@livelike.com)

***

## Opening the Genie Panel

Go to **Producer Suite → Scroll down to Business Tools → Genie tab**

***

## Using Genie to Manage Resources

### Programs

Ask:\
`List programs scheduled for today`
Genie returns program details using the Programs schema.

### Text Polls / Predictions / Quizzes

Genie supports the full lifecycle:\
**Create → Schedule → Publish**
Follows the standard widget creation flow.

***

## Giving Feedback

### Inline Feedback

1. Hit the Thumbs up/Thumbs down button.
2. Select the reason for feedback. If the reason is not listed, select. "Others"
3. Type something like:\
   `feedback: Genie didn’t list the right program.`
   and then Hit Submit.
