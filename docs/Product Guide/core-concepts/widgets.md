# Understanding LiveLike Widgets

LiveLike widgets are the centerpiece of the Engagement Suite. They transform viewing into participation by letting audiences vote, predict, react, and play along in real time. Whether you’re streaming live sports, running a trivia game, or publishing breaking updates, widgets give you the tools to make content interactive and memorable.

## What Are Widgets?

Widgets are interactive elements published during a live stream or on-demand video. They are:

* Ready-made engagement tools with built-in UX
* Easy to configure through the Producer Suite or via the REST API
* Flexible enough to fit into live streams, VOD, or standalone experiences
* Customizable to match your brand’s design and tone

Every widget is associated with a Program. Programs group multiple widgets around a piece of content and carry a unique Program ID. Programs also hold scheduling and metadata to help producers organize events inside the dashboard.

## Core Widget Types

Widgets fall into three broad categories:
**Interactive Widgets**

* Polls → Collect audience opinions in real time (text or images).
* Quizzes/Trivia → Test knowledge and reward correct answers.
* Predictions → Let viewers guess outcomes and reveal results later.
* Alerts → Deliver important updates, offers, or sponsor content.
* Cheer Meters → Capture live excitement by letting users tap to show support.
* Image Sliders → Measure sentiment or preference along a visual scale.

**Social Widgets**

* Chat → Enable public or group conversations.
* Comments → Collect open-ended thoughts or crowd questions.
* Reactions → Express emotions with instant feedback icons.

**Gamification Widgets**

* Leaderboards → Rank users based on participation or performance.
* Rewards → Distribute virtual goods or incentives.
* Achievements → Recognize milestones like streaks or event participation.

## Presentation Modes

How widgets appear in your app can be just as important as the content itself. LiveLike supports three main formats:

* Popup Widgets → Appear one at a time for a limited window. Great for polls, trivia, or quick predictions.
* Timeline Widgets → Display in a scrollable feed, letting audiences revisit past widgets. Ideal for live blogs, commentary, and multi-question games.
* Embedded Widgets → Added directly into web pages via embed codes or custom templates. Perfect for standalone or sponsor-driven activations.

## How Widgets Work

### Easy Implementation

* Quick setup via the Producer Suite (CMS)
* Can also be published through REST API
* Flexible deployment options with customization

### Real-Time Interaction

* Instant audience participation
* Live results and updates synchronized with the video
* Spoiler prevention built-in for seamless experiences

### Engagement Features

* Points and rewards systems to drive repeat usage
* Social sharing capabilities
* Full branding and customization support
* Analytics and tracking to measure performance
