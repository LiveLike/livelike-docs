Rewards in LiveLike are digital goods that users can earn, collect, and accumulate. They make your app more engaging, give users a sense of progress, and encourage long-term investment in your experience.
When paired with Leaderboards, rewards can also add a competitive and challenging layer that motivates users to keep coming back.
The Rewards system provides:

* Customization – design rewards to fit your experience
* Inventory management – track user balances and collections
* Automation – define rules so rewards are granted instantly
* Flexibility – support everything from XP systems to collectibles

## Rewards Components

The Rewards system is built from a few core components that you can mix and match to create the experience you want:
**Reward Items**
An Item is the blueprint of a reward. You can create as many as you like. Items can act like:

* Points or XP (accumulated values)
* Collectibles (badges, cards, tokens)

Users can collect and build up their items over time.

## Reward Actions

A Reward Action is something a user does that can earn them rewards.
Built-in actions include:

* Voting in a poll
* Answering a quiz correctly
* Replying to an Ask widget

You can also define custom actions for activities outside LiveLike, such as:

* Making a purchase
* Inviting a friend
* Completing a profile
* Watching a video

Once you recognize a user has completed the action, you can trigger it and deliver the rewards.

## Reward Tables

A Reward Table defines which items are earned from which actions. Each table entry links:

* **Action → Reward Item → Amount**

Whenever a user performs an action, all linked tables are checked, and matching rewards are granted automatically.

You can run multiple tables at once to support different scenarios. For example:

* Different sports, each with their own reward system
* Regular season vs. playoff rewards
* Special one-time events like tournaments or exhibitions

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  💡 Pro Tip: Use Reward Tables for recurring rewards (e.g., points for voting, referrals).
  For more complex, one-time incentives, use Quests to guide users through multi-step journeys.
</Callout>

## Putting It All Together

By combining Reward Items, Reward Actions, and Reward Tables, you can design a reward system that fits your app perfectly. Whether you want to track XP, offer collectibles, or create event-based incentives, LiveLike Rewards makes it easy to:

* Keep users engaged
* Encourage repeat interactions
* Build a sense of competition and accomplishment
