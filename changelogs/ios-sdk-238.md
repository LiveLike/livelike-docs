## Whats New?

* [Chat] Messaging Pinning - added `pinMessage`, `unpinMessage` and `getPinMessageInfoList` to `ChatSession`. See [https://docs.livelike.com/docs/pin-message](https://docs.livelike.com/docs/pin-message)
* [Rewards] PredictionWidgetModel now has an `earnableRewards` field which describes the rewards that can be earned from the Prediction.
