## Whats New?

* Added `WidgetTimelineViewController` - a quick way to realize a Timeline style presentation for your application. See [Widget Timeline View Controller](doc:widget-timeline-view-controller) for details!

> 🚧 With the addition of WidgetTimelineViewController we've renamed `WidgetViewController` to `WidgetPopupViewController` to be more representative of the presentation style and to be more distinct from `WidgetTimelineViewController`. You may see new compiler warning when updating to 2.20.
