## What's New?

* `Theme` now has an additional field `chatInputTextColor` allowing integrators to modify the text color of the text field in the `ChatInputView`
* Quote Message now supports Accessibility using VoiceOver
* Introducing [Quests](https://dash.readme.com/project/livelike/v1/docs/quests) - a new way to engage your users
